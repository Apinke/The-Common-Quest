The Commons Quest: Creative Commons for Everyone is an interactive, comic-style story that teaches Creative Commons through five characters. It’s built as a deck of nine flip cards, it’s  meant to be useful to anyone hearing about Creative Commons for the first time.
The story. Amina, a creator, wants to share her mural but still get credit. Tunde, a teacher, wants to use it in his class. Mama Grace, a librarian, wants her library to share its collections. 
Lockdown, the villain, insists that everything stay “all rights reserved.” Mister Byte, a guide robot, shows them the middle path. By the end, even Lockdown agrees that sharing smartly benefits everyone.
